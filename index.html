About Leona

2015

Leona Graves sees what others miss. Offer a name, and she uncovers the story behind it; clients return years later to confirm her predictions.

Practicing professionally since 2015, she pairs clairvoyance, tarot, oracle, and dream interpretation with a Bachelor of Science in Psychology and four and a half years of formal development. Each session weighs the people involved as closely as the cards. She writes fast and holds nothing back, so every minute carries substance.

She has worked the Sedona Psychic Fair and co-created Theta Wave Oracle, and more than 1,300 reviews stand behind her. Reserve your time and leave with clarity worth keeping.

Questions

Which reading suits my question? Each deck answers its own kind of question. Graves Tarot shows where a situation is heading; Graves Oracle shows what passes between two people; Falkor's Fortunes shows what the present moment asks. A guide to every reading type is on its way here.

How should I word my question? Plain facts and one clear aim. The more honest and specific the question, the more precise the answer.

How do I book? Live chat runs on Bitwine. Written readings open on Etsy. Readings are booked through those pages rather than by message.

How soon does a written reading arrive? Within 24 to 48 hours; an early purchase can arrive the same morning.
